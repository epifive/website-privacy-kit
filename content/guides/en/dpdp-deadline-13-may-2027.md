# DPDP deadline 13 May 2027: countdown and plan for small businesses

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 was passed in August 2023, but most of it did not start at once. The DPDP Rules, 2025 (G.S.R. 846(E), dated 13 November 2025) switch it on in phases. For an ordinary Indian business with a website, clinic register, admissions form or customer list, the date that matters is **13 May 2027**.

This page explains what started when, what is still uncertain, and a realistic month-by-month plan.

## What starts when

Rule 1 of the DPDP Rules sets three dates ([Rules PDF](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)):

| Phase | What comes into force | When |
|---|---|---|
| Rule 1(2) | Rules 1, 2 and 17–21: definitions and the Data Protection Board's machinery | On publication: 13/14 Nov 2025 |
| Rule 1(3) | Rule 4: registration and obligations of Consent Managers | One year later: Nov 2026 (now in force) |
| Rule 1(4) | Rules 3, 5–16, 22 and 23: notice, security, breach intimation, retention, contact person, children's data, rights, transfers and more | Eighteen months after publication: **13 May 2027** |

The matching sections of the Act (notice, consent, duties of Data Fiduciaries, rights of Data Principals and penalties) were notified to start on the same dates by a separate notification, G.S.R. 843(E). The PIB explainer of 17 Nov 2025 describes this as "an eighteen-month period for phased compliance" ([PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf)).

### 13 May or 14 May?

The Rules are signed "New Delhi, the 13th November, 2025", while the e-Gazette upload and the PIB explainer say 14 November 2025. Depending on which date you count from, eighteen months ends on 13 or 14 May 2027. **Plan for 13 May 2027.** Being ready a day early costs nothing.

### Until then

The Information Technology Act s.43A and the SPDI Rules, 2011 continue to apply until s.44(2) of the DPDP Act commences on 13 May 2027. The CERT-In Directions of 2022 (6-hour incident reporting, 180-day logs in India) already apply today. See [breach intimation: 72 hours and 6 hours](/dpdp/dpdp-breach-intimation-72-hours-6-hours).

## What is still uncertain

Treat these as open items and re-check them monthly:

- **A shorter deadline.** News reports in January 2026 said MeitY was considering cutting the 18 months to 12 ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/meity-may-cut-compliance-timeline-for-key-dpdp-rules-to-12-months-126012201293_1.html)). As of November 2026 we found **no notification** making that change. Check the e-Gazette (egazette.gov.in) and MeitY's [DPDP Rules page](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa).
- **The Board.** News reports in November 2026 said MeitY opened Consent Manager registration but the Board still had no appointed members ([ANI](https://www.aninews.in/news/business/govt-opens-registration-for-consent-managers-under-dpdp-act-rs-2-crore-net-worth-required20261114145151/), [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-companies-consent-managers-dpdp-act/)). The Board's complaint and breach-intimation channels are not yet confirmed.
- **Court challenge.** A challenge before the Supreme Court has been reported, with notice issued but no stay reported; a listing was reported for 17 Nov 2026 ([news report](https://dpdpactindia.in/blog/dpdp-13-november-2026-to-13-may-2027/)). Unless a court orders otherwise, the dates above stand.
- **Languages.** Section 5(3) asks for the option of English or any Eighth Schedule language. How many languages a small business needs is not settled.

None of these is a reason to wait. The duties themselves are already fixed in the Act and Rules.

## What a small business must have by 13 May 2027

1. **A standalone notice** with an itemised list of personal data and purposes, and links to withdraw consent, exercise rights and complain to the Board (s.5, Rule 3). See [privacy notice format in Hindi](/dpdp/dpdp-privacy-notice-format-hindi).
2. **Valid consent** where consent is the basis: clear, purpose-wise, no pre-ticked boxes, easy withdrawal (s.6). See [DPDP consent banner](/dpdp/dpdp-consent-banner).
3. **A published contact person** for privacy questions (s.8(9), Rule 9).
4. **A rights request route and response period**, not exceeding ninety days (Rule 14). See [request reply templates](/dpdp/data-principal-request-reply-templates).
5. **Reasonable security safeguards**, including access control, logs and backups, with logs and data kept for one year (Rule 6, Rule 8(3)).
6. **A breach plan** for intimation to affected people and the Board (Rule 7).
7. **Contracts with processors** such as your host, CRM and SMS or WhatsApp provider (s.8(2), Rule 6(1)(f)).
8. **Erasure** when consent is withdrawn or the purpose is over, unless the law needs you to keep the data (s.8(7)).
9. **Children's data rules** if you deal with anyone under 18 (s.9, Rule 10). See [DPDP for coaching centres](/dpdp/dpdp-for-coaching-centres).
10. **A notice to existing customers** whose consent you took before commencement, "as soon as it is reasonably practicable" (s.5(2)).

Things the law does **not** ask of ordinary SMBs: a Data Protection Officer (only notified Significant Data Fiduciaries, s.10), a fixed erasure period (only the large platforms in the Third Schedule), or storing data only in India.

## Month-by-month plan: December 2026 to May 2027

| Month | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Dec 2026** | Map your data | A list of every place you collect personal data (website forms, WhatsApp, billing, registers, Google Sheets), what data, why, and who else gets it |
| **Jan 2027** | Purposes and basis | For each purpose: consent or legitimate use (s.7); retention period and the law behind it; list of vendors and whether you have a contract |
| **Feb 2027** | Notices | Draft notice in English and Hindi; contact person; rights page; have a lawyer review |
| **Mar 2027** | Consent on the website and forms | Banner, preference centre, form checkboxes, consent records; test that Reject and Withdraw are as easy as Accept |
| **Apr 2027** | Processes | Request inbox with due dates; breach register and templates; staff walkthrough; processor contract clauses; existing-customer notice drafted |
| **1–12 May 2027** | Go live | Publish notice and rights page; switch on the banner; send the s.5(2) notice to existing customers; keep a dated record of everything |
| **After 13 May** | Run it | Answer requests on time; log incidents; review once a quarter; watch for Board guidance |

If you start later, compress January and February into two weeks. The most visible items, the notice, the banner and the contact person, can be done in a few days with templates.

## FAQ

**Is the DPDP Act already in force?**
Partly. The Board machinery has applied since November 2025 and Consent Manager registration since November 2026. The duties most businesses must follow start on 13 May 2027 (Rule 1(4)).

**Will the deadline be extended?**
No extension has been notified. The only change reported in the news was a proposal to shorten it, which had not been notified as of November 2026.

**Does it apply to a small shop or clinic?**
The Act has no size threshold for ordinary duties. It applies to digital personal data, including data collected on paper and later digitised (s.3). The exceptions are personal or domestic use and data the person has made public herself.

**What are the penalties after 13 May 2027?**
The Schedule to the Act sets maximums, for example "may extend to fifty crore rupees" for breaches not listed separately. The Board imposes a penalty only after an inquiry (s.28) finds a significant breach (s.33). Penalty is not automatic.

**Do I need to register anywhere?**
Ordinary businesses do not register with the Board. Registration is for Consent Managers (Rule 4). See [Consent Manager vs consent tool](/dpdp/consent-manager-vs-consent-tool).
